# About this brief

**Who it's for.** Priya Raman, General Manager of Customer Experience at Capital Bank of Australia, a fictional bank. Reads it on Monday morning before the executive meeting. Everything here is generated demo data (`demo/make_data.py`).

**What matters.** Anything that stops customers paying, logging in or getting their money, and anything that could mean customer harm: declined cards, lockouts, delayed transfers, unexpected fees. Scam reports are always important, even when there are only a few. Separate problems from requests and praise. Call out anything tied to an app version, a region or a recent change.

**Tone.** Plain, measured and specific. Say when the evidence is thin. No marketing language.

**Look.** Deep navy and gold. Call it the Weekly Customer Feedback Brief.

**Standing instructions.** Tickets go to the owning team: card fraud operations, digital banking, payments, pricing, or the scams team.
